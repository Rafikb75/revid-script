Most AI video generators force creators into complex timelines or pure diffusion prompting where character consistency is a gamble. Revid AI takes the opposite approach: it is an algorithmic vertical video assembler engineered strictly for social media algorithms (TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts).

01

02

Converts the finalized script into naturalistic voiceover with zero robotic cadence, calibrated with micro-pauses at key punctuation marks.
